Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026

Decor Areas Studio is an independently branded content marketing, creative and analytics tool for creators and businesses.

Pinterest authorization

Users may connect their own Pinterest account through Pinterest's official OAuth authorization flow. Decor Areas Studio does not request or collect Pinterest passwords or Pinterest session cookies.

API data

Decor Areas Studio fetches information authorized by the user, such as account information, Boards, Pins and organic analytics, only as needed to provide requested functionality. It does not use unauthorized scraping or automated extraction from Pinterest.

User content and publishing

Users may provide images, titles, descriptions, destination URLs and related content. Users review and explicitly choose each Pin they publish.

Relationship with Pinterest

Decor Areas Studio is an independent product. It is not an official Pinterest product and does not claim Pinterest Business Partner status unless such status is separately granted by Pinterest.

Revocation and deletion

Users can revoke Pinterest authorization in their Pinterest settings. See our Data Deletion & Disconnection page for additional information.
