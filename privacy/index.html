Privacy Policy

Effective: September 24, 2026

Decor Areas Studio is an independent content publishing, scheduling and analytics service for creators and businesses.

Workspace accounts

If you create a workspace account, we store the email address you provide, a one-way password hash, session records, plan status and identifiers required to provide the service.

Pinterest authorization

Users connect their own Pinterest account through Pinterest's OAuth Authorization Code flow. We do not request or collect Pinterest passwords or Pinterest session cookies. OAuth access and refresh credentials are encrypted and used only to provide authorized features.

Pinterest API information

When requested, the service fetches the authorized account's profile information, Boards, Pins and organic analytics from Pinterest. The visual Pin library and analytics views are fetched from the API on demand rather than maintained as a cross-account Pinterest data warehouse. Scheduler records contain the user-provided publishing content, the selected target Board reference, the requested publishing time and operational status required to execute the future action that the user approved.

Website and WordPress import

Users may save a public website URL and import published content from a public WordPress REST feed or public article URL. Public page metadata may include the article title, description, canonical URL and Open Graph image. We do not request a WordPress password for this public-content workflow.

AI-assisted features

When an AI provider is configured, user-supplied topics, article text, brand instructions or prompts may be sent to that provider to generate editable text or images. Pinterest API-derived account, Pin, Board or analytics data is not used to train, fine-tune or improve AI models through Decor Areas Studio and is not intentionally sent as AI-generation input.

Payments

If paid checkout is enabled, payment processing is handled by the configured payment provider. Decor Areas Studio stores only the customer/subscription identifiers and plan status needed to provide the service; card details are handled by the payment provider.

What we do not do

We do not scrape Pinterest, collect Pinterest login credentials, sell Pinterest API data, provide unauthorized competitor research or benchmarking, combine unrelated users' Pinterest information into a data product, use Pinterest information for off-Pinterest ad targeting, or use Pinterest Materials to train or improve AI models.

User control

Users can disconnect Pinterest in Settings, revoke authorization through Pinterest, remove saved website connections, cancel queued Pins and delete their workspace account. See Data Deletion & Disconnection.

Independent product

Decor Areas Studio is independently branded. It is not an official Pinterest product and does not claim Pinterest Business Partner status unless separately granted.
