Data Deletion & Disconnection

Decor Areas Studio provides controls for Pinterest authorization, saved websites, scheduler data and workspace accounts.

Disconnect Pinterest

Open Studio → Settings and choose Disconnect browser. You may also revoke the app directly in Pinterest account settings.

Cancel scheduled Pins

Open Scheduler and cancel an item that has not yet been published. Cancelled items are not processed as due scheduled Pins.

Remove a website connection

Open Blog → Pin and remove a saved public website connection. The saved URL is removed from the workspace database.

Delete the workspace account

Settings includes Delete workspace account. Deletion removes the account and its cascading application sessions, stored Pinterest authorization record, saved website connections and scheduler records from the connected database.

Pinterest library and analytics

The visual Pin library, Boards and account analytics are requested from Pinterest on demand and are not maintained as a cross-account Pinterest data warehouse by this build.

Never send passwords, access tokens, refresh tokens or application secrets in a support or deletion request.
